California

California is on the west coast of the United States. It has large cities with lots of people but it also has some great natural areas. One example is Death Valley. The lowest elevation in the United States is located there. There are great places to look around there and also hike. But don't hike in the summer... It is super hot! The sand dunes there are very spectacular.
