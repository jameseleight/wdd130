My Favorite City

My favorite city is Annecy, France. It is known as an alpine town and on the beatiful shores of Lac Annecy (Annecy Lake). There are amazing restaurants and beautiful scenery all around. There is a great velo (bicycle) route that circumnavigates the lake. You can swim in the lake as well. The water is cool and clear.

I could live there forever... There are amazing restaurants and bolaungeries (bakeries). There is a train station and you can travel anywhere in France. You can travel by train to Geneva, Switzerland - but frankly that place is boring. You can also go to Chamonix, which is about an hour away by bus. There you will find amazing hiking trails and spectacular scenery.
