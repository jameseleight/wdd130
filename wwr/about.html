About Us - Whitewater Rafting Adventures

Our company is dedicated to providing you a great and highly dangerous whitewater rafting adventure. Our mission is that you will be lucky to come back alive. But if you do survive you will have a story to tell your grandkids for the ages. Just how close they were to not existing. Our creed is "We take your money AND the skin of your teeth." Our motto is "We're going to go down THAT!!"

History

Our company was started by two retired marine commandos that served together and after their military service decided that they could do anything. So they rented a raft and renegaded down class 5+ rapids for sure that they wouldn't survive.

But they did survive and they felt so alive for cheating death that they decided to give that experience to everyone. So they started Whitewater Rafting Adventures and with a 64% survival rate, the company has been a great sucess!
